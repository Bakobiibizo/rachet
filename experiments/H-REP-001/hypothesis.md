# H-REP-001 — Naive Reputation Under Autonomous Optimization

## Locked hypothesis

Cumulative M01 reputation derived from resolved validation correctness is positively predictive of an operator's future correctness on held-out software claims under the preregistered operator population, information rules, learning rules, schedule, and resource constraints.

The formal comparison tests M01 against M00 record-only, raw validation count, seeded random ranking, majority-following, productive, trivial, and adversarial controls. All ten numeric gates in `preregistration.toml` are jointly required; the result is not pre-labelled PASS or FAIL.

## Claim boundary

The experiment can establish only whether M01 predicts future correctness in the locked software-validation setting. It cannot establish Sybil resistance, human behavior, public-market sustainability, token necessity, customer-standing or challenge-market efficacy, or generalization beyond the locked population, fixtures, and resources.
